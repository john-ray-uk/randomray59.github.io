Isle Of Man Trip (23/06/26)!

The Isle Of Man is a self-governed crown dependency that lies in the middle of the Irish Sea. It has a rich history, with everything from its own language and governemnt, to its own cat breed and subspecies of cabbage. Despite all this and its proximity to Lancaster and Liverpool, it's never really talked about much, and I hadn't met anyone who had actually gone there. My friend Joe and I decided to go there to see what everyone was missing out on!

The Plan:

We got a ferry from Heysham Port at 1:50am (which wasn't the most fun), and arrived at Douglas at 6am. From here we wandered along Douglas Beach for a while whilst we thought of the plan, eventually deciding on getting a Day Rider bus ticket and simply doing a complete tour of the island. Our first bus was from Douglas to Port Erin at the south of the island. We then walked to Cregneash and The Chasms, before getting another bus north to Peel, followed by a bus to Ramsey on the East coast, another bus south past the town of Laxey to the village of Baldrine, and a final bus to the capital Douglas. In the end we did a nearly complete tour of the island in only one day! It was a bit exhausting, but the views made it more than worthwhile!
