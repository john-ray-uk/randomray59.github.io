Alicante (08/01/26)!

On the final day of our trip, we got up early to go to the Alicante docks and catch a ferry to the island of Tabarca. There weren't many birds there, but the views were incredible and the weather was perfect. Afterwards, we visited The Pink Lake Of Torrevieja, before spending the rest of the day at the Clot de Galvany - the nature reserve where we started our trip.
