Nature Photography

Here are some of my best nature photographs. The rest of my nature photography can be seen in the "All Photos" section of this website, which includes photos of things other than birds! These were all taken with my Nikon D3300 camera, with either my AF-P Nikkor 18-55mm DX lens, AF-P Nikkor 70-300mm DX-VR lens, or my newer Sigma 150-600mm DG lens. This page is currently a work-in-progress; many more photos will be uploaded onto here soon.
